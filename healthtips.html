Health Tips

Simple Tips From Our Doctors To Keep You & Your Family Healthy Every Day.

1. HOW TO PREVENT MALARIA

Dr. Rubangakene Julius Welbon: Malaria is still the #1 killer in Uganda, but it is 100% preventable.

✅ Sleep under a treated mosquito net EVERY night
✅ Close windows before 6pm
✅ Clear stagnant water around your home - mosquitoes breed there
✅ If you feel fever, headache, joint pain - come for a malaria test within 24 hours. Don't wait!

Malaria Test at Rubaa: 10,000 UGX - Result in 20 Minutes

2. FOR PREGNANT MOTHERS

Dr. Sarah Nakato: A healthy mother = A healthy baby.

✅ Come for antenatal check-up at least 4 times
✅ Eat green vegetables, beans, eggs, and fruits every day
✅ Sleep on your left side for better blood flow to baby
✅ Drink 8 glasses of water daily
✅ Don't take medicine without asking a doctor

Antenatal at Ferara: 30,000 UGX - Includes Ultrasound

3. FOR CHILDREN - KEEP YOUR CHILD STRONG

Dr. Musa Kato: Most child sickness comes from dirty hands and dirty water.

✅ Wash hands with soap before eating and after toilet
✅ Give your child clean boiled water only
✅ Immunize your child - It's FREE every Wednesday at Ferara
✅ Give fruits, vegetables, and porridge with groundnuts
✅ If child has fever + vomiting + diarrhoea - bring them FAST. Don't wait 3 days.

4. HOW TO PREVENT DIABETES & PRESSURE

Dr. Rubangakene Julius Welbon: Diabetes and Pressure are now killing many Ugandans under 40. They are silent killers.

✅ Reduce sugar, soda, and too much salt
✅ Walk for 30 minutes every day
✅ Eat more vegetables, less fried food
✅ Check your blood pressure every 3 months - It's FREE at Ferara
✅ Stop smoking and reduce alcohol

5. HOW TO KEEP YOUR TEETH CLEAN

Dr. Grace Atim: Toothache is painful, but you can prevent it.

✅ Brush teeth 2 times a day - morning and before sleep
✅ Use fluoride toothpaste
✅ Don't eat too many sweets and soda
✅ Visit dentist every 6 months for cleaning

Teeth Cleaning at Ferara: 15,000 UGX Only

6. DRINK WATER - YOUR BODY NEEDS IT

✅ Drink 8 glasses of clean water daily
✅ Water prevents headache, constipation, and kidney stones
✅ Avoid taking too many energy drinks and sodas
