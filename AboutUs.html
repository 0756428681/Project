About Rubaa Hospital

We Don't Just Treat Sickness, We Care For Life.

OUR STORY

Rubaa Hospital was founded by Frank Rijkaard. It is located at Plot 12, Kampala Road, Kampala, Uganda.

We started in 2022 as a small clinic with only 5 staff members and 1 small room. Our dream was simple: To give every Ugandan quality healthcare with love and respect, at a price they can afford.

Today, Rubaa Hospital has grown to over 50 staff members and we serve more than 500 patients every week. But our heart has not changed.

OUR MISSION

To provide quality, affordable, patient-centered healthcare 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, with compassion and respect for every life.

OUR VISION

To become Uganda's most trusted hospital - where every patient feels cared for, safe, and valued.

OUR CORE VALUES

1. Compassion: We treat you like family.
2. Integrity: We are honest. Genuine drugs only, no fake medicine.
3. Excellence: We use modern equipment and experienced doctors.
4. Teamwork: Doctors, nurses and patients work together.
5. Respect: Every patient, rich or poor, gets the same care.

OUR NUMBERS
